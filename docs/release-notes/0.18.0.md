**Jarvis settles in: a real welcome, a memory, safer builds, and an AI option
with no keys at all.** Same three files as before — pick the one for your
server.

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `jarvis-paper-0.18.0.jar` | Paper / Purpur 1.21.11 – 26.2 (needs Citizens) |
| `jarvis-fabric-0.18.0.jar` | Fabric, Minecraft 26.3 (needs Fabric API) |
| `jarvis-neoforge-0.18.0.jar` | NeoForge 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 |

### Upgrading from 0.17

- Replace the old jar with the new one. Your config and data carry over.
- `build.undo-timeout` and `build.max-undo-size` were never read and are
  replaced by `build.undo-history` (default 10).
- New settings are documented in place in `config.yml` and in
  [docs/configuration.md](https://github.com/iamgadgetman/jarvis/blob/main/docs/configuration.md).

### New

- **AI in one command, no keys.** `/jarvis cloud activate` puts a link in
  chat; open it, press Activate, and Jarvis sets himself up on Jarvis Cloud.
  Every server gets 125 free requests a month and a two-week trial at 300.
  Paid plans start at $15 a year: [jarvis-cloud.app](https://jarvis-cloud.app).
  Entirely optional — your own key and Ollama work exactly as before, and the
  plugin never contacts Jarvis Cloud unless you run that command.
- **A proper welcome.** New players get the controller bell and an offer of a
  short tutorial that reacts to what they actually do. Admins on a server with
  no AI get three clickable ways to fix it: free local Ollama, a free Gemini
  key, or Jarvis Cloud. `/jarvis setup` checks again.
- **He remembers.** Conversations and notable events are kept per player and
  brought into his replies, so "where did I say my base was?" works after a
  restart. `/jarvis memory` shows what he knows; `/jarvis memory forget` wipes it.
- **He knows where you are.** Requests carry your health, food, held item,
  biome, nearby mobs and what he is doing himself, so "what should I do?" gets
  a real answer. Put server facts and named places in `knowledge.yml`.
- **Builds preview first.** An AI build shows a particle outline and waits for
  yes. Dangerous blocks are refused, size and distance are capped, and each
  permission group gets a cooldown and a daily budget.
- **One undo for everything.** `/jarvis undo` takes down the last job — AI
  build, shape or schematic paste — and survives a restart. `/jarvis undo list`
  and `/jarvis redo` too.
- **No summons needed.** Ask for a job and he comes: "On my way, sir."
- **Reward bundles.** Admins define named command bundles; Jarvis can hand one
  out when what a player says fits, with once-per-player and cooldown rules.
- **Faster.** Simple orders ("follow me", "stop", "mine 10 iron") run with no
  AI call, replies appear sentence by sentence, and he shows he's thinking.

### Fixed

- The night skips with Jarvis out, and on the mods he no longer appears in
  the tab list, `/list` or the server's player count.
- No more spare Jarvises after a crash.
- He no longer changes the time or weather, hands out free items, teleports
  you or announces to everyone unless you asked.
- One reply per order, no blank replies.
- Builds get real glass windows and sensible chairs.
